    The recipe

    log P(k) = log prior(k) + Σᵢ wᵢ · log(qᵢ(k) / prior(k)), wᵢ = chef weight × trust(up|down) × 0.5^(age / half-life)

    Half-lives: 2, 7 and 20 days for the 5d, 22d and 66d horizons. Chef weight starts at 0.5, from a Beta(2,2) prior on hit rate, until calls are graded.
