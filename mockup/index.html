    The recipe

    log P(k) = log prior(k) + Σᵢ wᵢ · log(qᵢ(k) / prior(k)), wᵢ = chef weight × trust(up|down) × 0.5^(age / half-life)

    Half-lives: 2 days for the 5-day horizon, 7 days for 22 days, 20 days for 66 days. Chef weight starts from a Beta(2,2) prior on hit rate.
