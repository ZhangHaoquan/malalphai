    The recipe, in one line

    log P(k) = log prior(k) + Σ wᵢ · log(qᵢ(k) / prior(k))

    k is down, sideways or up. qᵢ is Gemini's reading of dish i, and wᵢ = chef weight × your up/down trust × freshness.

    Freshness halves after 2 days for the 5-day call, 7 days for the 22-day call and 20 days for the 66-day call. Each chef's weight starts from a Beta(2,2) prior, so a new chef starts at 50%.
